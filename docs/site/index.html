About MangaLord

Your library, your pace.

MangaLord is a cross-platform manga client built for discovering content, reading chapters, browsing anime, and organizing the stories you want to return to.

Less noise. More stories.

Search, discover, open a chapter, and keep your library organized in one dark, comfortable reading space.

What you can do

Made for discovery.

A calm set of tools for finding, following, and returning to manga and anime content.

01

Search & discover

Explore available manga and anime sources and find content through the app’s search and discovery views.

02

Read chapters

Open chapters in the existing reader with reading progress and configurable reading preferences.

03

Watch episodes

Browse anime content and use the existing media player for supported episodes.

04

Build a library

Save favorites and revisit your history, either locally as a guest or synchronized with an account.

05

Read offline

Download supported chapters and manage your offline reading queue from the app.

06

Choose your setup

Adjust language, notifications, source selections, reader behavior, and playback preferences.
